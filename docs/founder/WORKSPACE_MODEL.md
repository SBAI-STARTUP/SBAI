# Purpose

The Workspace Model defines how the Founder interacts with SBAI through logical workspaces.

Each workspace groups related capabilities into a focused environment while sharing a common architecture, navigation model, and enterprise knowledge.

---

# Design Principles

- Single unified platform
- One shared knowledge base
- Context-aware AI assistance
- Modular workspace architecture
- Consistent user experience
- Secure by design

---

# Core Workspaces

## Dashboard

Enterprise overview, recent activity, alerts, metrics, and quick actions.

---

## Knowledge

Documentation, research, enterprise memory, architecture, and knowledge graph.

---

## Architecture

Enterprise architecture, ADRs, system models, diagrams, and standards.

---

## Projects

Planning, milestones, roadmaps, execution tracking, and project management.

---

## Products

Product ideas, requirements, specifications, releases, and lifecycle management.

---

## AI

AI agents, prompts, workflows, reasoning, orchestration, and model management.

---

## Security

Security posture, audit logs, policies, secrets, vulnerabilities, and compliance.

---

## Infrastructure

Cloud resources, deployments, services, monitoring, networking, and automation.

---

## Research

Experiments, prototypes, future technologies, evaluations, and innovation tracking.

---

## Settings

Platform configuration, integrations, preferences, identifiers, and governance settings.

---

# Workspace Relationships

All workspaces share:

- Enterprise Knowledge
- AI Services
- Authentication
- Navigation
- Search
- Notifications

Each workspace provides specialized functionality while remaining integrated into the Founder Platform.

---

# Future Expansion

New workspaces may be introduced through an approved Architecture Decision Record (ADR) when justified by platform growth.
