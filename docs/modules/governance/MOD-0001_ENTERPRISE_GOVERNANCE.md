# Purpose

Define the governance model of SBAI.

This module establishes decision authority, governance responsibilities, architectural ownership, and enterprise-level policies that guide the evolution of SBAI.

---

# Scope

Applies to every engineering artifact, repository, service, AI system, application, product, and future business unit within SBAI.

---

# Governance Principles

- Founder-directed governance
- Documentation-first engineering
- Architecture before implementation
- Security by design
- Traceable decisions through ADRs
- Version-controlled documentation

---

# Decision Authority

Strategic decisions require Founder approval.

AI systems may analyze, recommend, and automate approved work but do not independently change enterprise governance.
