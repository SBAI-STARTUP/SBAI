---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official taxonomy of all enterprise capabilities within SBAI.

The taxonomy provides a structured classification that ensures every capability belongs to a defined domain and supports traceability, governance, implementation planning, and future expansion.

---

# 2. Taxonomy Principles

Every capability shall:

- Have a unique identifier
- Belong to exactly one primary domain
- Support traceability
- Be reusable
- Be independently governable
- Support future expansion
