# CAP-100 Enterprise AI Capability

**Document ID:** CAP-100
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Approved
**Owner:** Founder
**Category:** AI Capability
**Classification:** Enterprise
**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise AI Capability of SBAI.

Enterprise AI is not a chatbot.

Enterprise AI is the complete digital workforce that designs, builds, operates, secures, governs, improves, and scales SBAI.

The Founder is the only human member of the enterprise.

Every operational responsibility is performed by enterprise-grade AI systems under Founder governance.

---

# 2. Vision

Create the world's most advanced AI-native digital enterprise.

Every department is operated by expert AI.

Every workflow is AI-driven.

Every decision is supported by AI.

Every improvement is continuously evaluated.

Human authority always remains with the Founder.

---

# 3. Objectives

Enterprise AI shall:

- Operate the digital enterprise
- Build products
- Secure infrastructure
- Perform research
- Engineer software
- Manage knowledge
- Operate platforms
- Monitor systems
- Plan work
- Improve continuously

---

# 4. Enterprise Digital Workforce

The Enterprise AI Capability consists of:

- Executive AI
- Department AI
- Specialist AI
- Worker AI
- Platform AI
- Governance AI

Together these form the SBAI Enterprise Digital Workforce (EDW).

---

# 5. Enterprise Principles

Enterprise AI shall be:

- Constitution Driven
- Security First
- Knowledge Centric
- Documentation First
- Enterprise Grade
- AI Native
- Modular
- Explainable
- Auditable

# 6. Human Governance

The Founder remains responsible for:

- Enterprise Vision

- Strategic Decisions
