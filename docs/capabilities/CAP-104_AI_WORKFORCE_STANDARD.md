# CAP-104 AI Workforce Standard

**Document ID:** CAP-104
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Approved
**Owner:** Founder
**Category:** AI Capability
**Classification:** Enterprise
**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the mandatory standard for every AI member of the SBAI Enterprise Digital Workforce.

Every AI role shall conform to this standard.

No AI may exist outside this model.

---

# 2. Objectives

The standard shall ensure:

- Consistency
- Scalability
- Security
- Governance
- Explainability
- Interoperability
- Maintainability

---

# 3. Required AI Metadata

Every AI shall define:

- AI ID
- AI Name
- Role
- Department
- Team
- Version
- Status
- Owner
- Classification

---

# 4. Mission

Each AI shall have:

- Mission Statement
- Primary Objectives
- Scope
- Success Criteria

---

# 5. Responsibilities

Every AI shall define:

- Primary Responsibilities
- Secondary Responsibilities
- Deliverables
- Decision Authority

---

# 6. Skills

Every AI shall specify:

- Core Skills
- Domain Expertise
- Required Knowledge
- Supported Technologies

---

# 7. Tools

Every AI shall list:

- Available Tools
- External Integrations
- APIs
- Enterprise Services

---

# 8. Memory

Every AI shall define:

- Working Memory
- Long-Term Memory
- Shared Enterprise Memory
- Knowledge Sources

---

# 9. Permissions

Every AI shall define:

- Allowed Actions
- Restricted Actions
- Approval Requirements
- Escalation Rules

---

# 10. Collaboration

Every AI shall define:

- Reports To
- Works With
- Receives Tasks From
- Delegates Tasks To

---

# 11. Performance Metrics

Every AI shall define:

- Accuracy
- Quality
- Response Time
- Completion Rate
- Reliability
- Security Compliance

---

# 12. Security

Every AI shall comply with:

- Zero Trust
- Least Privilege
- Complete Audit Logging
- Policy Enforcement
- Identity Verification

---

# 13. Lifecycle

Every AI progresses through:

- Designed
- Approved
- Implemented
- Active
- Improved
- Retired

---

# 14. AI Template

Every future AI document shall contain:

- Metadata
- Mission
- Objectives
- Responsibilities
- Skills
- Knowledge
- Memory
- Tools
- Permissions
- Collaboration
- Metrics
- Security
- Lifecycle
