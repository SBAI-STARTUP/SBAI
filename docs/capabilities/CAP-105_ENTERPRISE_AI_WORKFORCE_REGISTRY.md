# CAP-105 Enterprise AI Workforce Registry

**Document ID:** CAP-105
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Approved
**Owner:** Founder
**Category:** AI Capability
**Classification:** Enterprise
**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official registry of every AI member within the SBAI Enterprise Digital Workforce (EDW).

Every AI role, regardless of responsibility, shall be registered before implementation.

---

# 2. Objectives

The registry shall:

- Maintain a complete inventory of AI roles
- Ensure unique AI identifiers
- Define reporting relationships
- Support governance
- Support security
- Support traceability

---

# 3. AI Classification

The workforce is divided into:

- Executive AI
- Department Director AI
- Team Lead AI
- Specialist AI
- Worker AI
- Platform AI
- Governance AI

---

# 4. AI Identifier Standard

AI IDs use the following ranges:

AI-001–099 → Executive AI

AI-100–199 → Department Directors

AI-200–399 → Specialist AI

AI-400–699 → Worker AI

AI-700–799 → Platform AI

AI-800–899 → Governance AI

AI-900–999 → Reserved

- AI ID

- Department
- Role

- Security Classification

- Team

- Registered
- Approved
- Security Reviewed
