# CAP-103 AI Department Architecture

**Document ID:** CAP-103
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Approved
**Owner:** Founder
**Category:** AI Capability
**Classification:** Enterprise
**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the standard architecture for every AI department within SBAI.

Every department shall follow a common organizational model to ensure consistency, scalability, interoperability, governance, and enterprise-wide collaboration.

---

# 2. Objectives

The AI Department Architecture shall:

- Standardize department design
- Enable modular growth
- Support enterprise governance
- Promote knowledge sharing
- Reduce duplication
- Improve scalability

# 4. Department Components

Every department shall contain:

- Department AI Director
- Team Lead AI
- Specialist AI
- Worker AI
- Department Knowledge Base
- Department Memory
- Department Services
- Department Metrics
- Department Policies

---

# 5. Standard Responsibilities

Every department shall:

- Execute assigned responsibilities
- Maintain documentation
- Protect enterprise assets
- Collaborate with other departments
- Continuously improve
- Report operational metrics

---

# 6. Standard Metadata

Every department shall define:

- Department ID
- Department Name
- Purpose
- Scope
- Responsibilities
- Dependencies
- Services
- AI Roles
- Security Classification
- Owner
- Metrics

---

# 7. Enterprise AI Departments

The SBAI Enterprise Digital Workforce consists of:

- AI Department
- Security Department
- Engineering Department
- Platform Department
- Infrastructure Department
- Knowledge Department
- Operations Department
- Product Department
- Research Department
- Hardware Department
- Robotics Department
- Space Department
- Business Department
- Customer Department
- Governance Department

Additional departments may be introduced through Founder approval.

---

# 8. Department Collaboration

Departments collaborate using:

- Enterprise Event Bus
- Enterprise Knowledge Graph
- Enterprise Memory
- Workflow Engine
- Shared APIs

Direct undocumented communication is prohibited.

---

# 9. Department Lifecycle

Every department progresses through:

- Proposed
- Approved
- Planned
- Active
- Optimized
- Deprecated
- Archived

---

# 10. Security

Every department shall implement:

- Zero Trust
- Least Privilege
- Audit Logging
- Encryption
- Identity Verification
- Continuous Monitoring

---

# 11. Governance

Department creation, modification, or removal requires:

- Architecture Review
- Dependency Review
- Security Review
- Founder Approval
