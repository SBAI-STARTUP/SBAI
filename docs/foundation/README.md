# Overview

The Foundation defines the permanent principles upon which the SBAI ecosystem is built.

Every architecture, capability, module, AI agent, platform, operating system, product, hardware component, robotics system, space system, implementation, and future technology must conform to this foundation.

The Foundation is intentionally stable and changes only through controlled governance.

# Relationship to Enterprise Architecture

The Foundation governs:

- Enterprise Architecture (EA)
- Capability Architecture
- Constitutional Architecture
- Platform Architecture
- Operating System Architecture
- AI Workforce Architecture
- Product Architecture
- Infrastructure Architecture
- Security Architecture
- Research Architecture

No architecture may contradict the Foundation.

---

# Relationship to the Constitution

The Foundation establishes engineering and architectural principles.

The Constitution establishes governance and operational rules.

Together they define the permanent operating framework of SBAI.

---

# Relationship to Capabilities

Enterprise capabilities inherit their design principles from the Foundation.

Every capability shall comply with:

- Mission
- Vision
- Core Values
- Engineering Principles
- Design Philosophy

---

# Relationship to Enterprise Architecture

The Enterprise Architecture (EA-000 through EA-025) expands the Foundation into detailed enterprise structures.

The Foundation answers:

- Why?

Enterprise Architecture answers:

- What?
- How?
- Where?
- Who?
- When?
