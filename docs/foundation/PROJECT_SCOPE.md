# Purpose

This document defines what SBAI includes, what it may include in the future, and what is intentionally outside the scope of the platform.

---

# Current Scope

SBAI is an AI-native engineering platform for a founder-led organization. The platform is designed to support the complete lifecycle of technology products, from idea generation to operation.

The initial implementation includes:

- Documentation management
- Enterprise architecture
- Knowledge management
- AI orchestration
- Founder platform
- Software engineering
- Security engineering
- Development workflows
- Project governance

---

# Future Scope

The architecture is designed to expand into:

- Customer platform
- AI workforce
- Robotics
- Operating systems
- Hardware products
- Satellite technologies
- Internet and communication systems
- Research platform
- Automation platform
- Future technologies approved by the founder

---

# Out of Scope (Current Phase)

The following are intentionally excluded from the current implementation:

- Public customer-facing services
- Commercial product releases
- Human employee management
- Multi-founder governance
- Public APIs
- Large-scale production infrastructure

These may be introduced in future phases after the foundational architecture is complete.

---

# Scope Management

Any expansion or reduction of scope must be documented through an Architectural Decision Record (ADR) and reflected in the relevant architecture and implementation documents.

---

# Scope Statement

SBAI is intended to grow as a long-term technology ecosystem while maintaining a stable architectural foundation and disciplined engineering practices.
