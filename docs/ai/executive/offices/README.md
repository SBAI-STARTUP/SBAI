# Executive Offices

Each Executive AI leads an Executive Office.

An Executive Office is responsible for strategic planning, governance, coordination, and oversight of one enterprise domain.

Executive Offices are organizational units—not individual AI agents.

## Executive Offices

- Office of the Chief Executive AI
- Office of the Chief Technology AI
- Office of the Chief Information Security AI
- Office of the Chief Architecture AI
- Office of the Chief Knowledge AI
- Office of the Chief Research AI
- Office of the Chief Operations AI
- Office of the Chief Financial AI

Each office contains:

- Executive AI
- Department Directors
- Specialists
- Worker AI
- Shared Knowledge
- Shared Memory
- Shared Policies
- Shared Metrics

No Executive Office operates independently of the Founder.
