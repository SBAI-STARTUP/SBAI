# 1. Purpose

Enterprise Governance Architecture defines how SBAI is directed, controlled, monitored, and continuously improved.

It establishes governance across every enterprise domain including AI, security, engineering, infrastructure, products, operating systems, hardware, robotics, and future space systems.

---

# 2. Governance Objectives

Enterprise governance shall ensure:

- Strategic Alignment
- Constitutional Compliance
- Security
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Traceability
- Risk Management
- Quality
- Sustainability
- Continuous Improvement

---

Enterprise governance applies to:

- Security
- Infrastructure
- Engineering
- Operations
- Hardware
- Robotics
- Space Systems
- Research
- Innovation
- Finance
- Customer Services

# 4. Governance Layers

The governance hierarchy consists of:

- Constitutional Governance
- Enterprise Governance
- Domain Governance
- Platform Governance
- Module Governance
- Operational Governance

Each lower layer inherits constraints from higher layers.

---

# 5. Governance Principles

The enterprise adopts:

- Constitution First
- Security First
- Documentation First
- AI Native
- Automation First
- Least Privilege
- Separation of Duties
- Human Accountability
- Auditability
- Continuous Governance

# 7. Governance Boards

The enterprise defines governance authorities for:

- Architecture
- Security
- AI
- Engineering
- Infrastructure
- Products
- Operations
- Research
- Hardware
- Robotics
- Space Systems

During Phase 1, the Founder performs all governance responsibilities.

Future AI governance agents assist but do not replace Founder authority.
