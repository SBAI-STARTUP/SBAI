# 1. Purpose

Enterprise Principles define the permanent architectural rules governing every decision made within SBAI.

They are mandatory for every:

- Platform
- Product
- Service
- Module
- AI Agent
- Operating System
- Hardware System
- Robotics System
- Space System

---

# 2. Core Principles

## AI First

Artificial Intelligence shall augment every major enterprise capability.

---

## Constitution Driven

All enterprise decisions shall comply with the SBAI Constitution.

---

## Documentation First

No implementation without documentation.

---

## Security by Design

Security is designed into every layer.

---

## Modular by Default

Every component shall have one clear responsibility.

---

## Capability Driven

Enterprise capabilities determine implementation priorities.

---

## Knowledge Centric

Knowledge is a strategic enterprise asset.

---

## Platform First

Shared platforms are preferred over isolated solutions.

---

## Automation First

Repetitive work shall be automated whenever practical.

---

## Scalability

Architectures must support future growth without redesign.

---

## Sustainability

Engineering decisions should remain valuable for years.

---

## Traceability

Every decision shall be traceable to architecture and documentation.

---

# 3. Engineering Rules

Engineering shall always prioritize:

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Performance
- Security
- Observability
- Reusability

---

# 4. Governance Rules

Every major decision requires:

- Documentation
- Review
- Versioning
- Traceability
- Architectural Compliance

---

# 5. Architectural Compliance

Every enterprise artifact shall comply with:

- Foundation
- Enterprise Architecture
- Capability Framework
- Constitution
- Standards
