# 1. Purpose

Enterprise Information Architecture defines how information is created, organized, shared, secured, governed, and consumed across SBAI.

Information Architecture focuses on business meaning rather than storage technology.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Information Architecture shall:

- Standardize enterprise information.
- Eliminate information silos.
- Improve information quality.
- Support enterprise decision making.
- Enable AI reasoning.
- Preserve enterprise knowledge.
- Improve interoperability.

---

# 3. Information Domains

Enterprise information includes:

- Business Information
- Architecture Information
- Capability Information
- Governance Information
- Security Information
- Engineering Information
- Product Information
- Customer Information
- AI Information
- Research Information
- Infrastructure Information
- Operational Information
- Financial Information
- Legal Information
- Hardware Information
- Robotics Information
- Space Information

---

# 4. Information Lifecycle

Information progresses through:

1. Creation
2. Validation
3. Classification
4. Storage
5. Usage
6. Sharing
7. Versioning
8. Archival
9. Retirement

---

# 5. Information Principles

Enterprise information shall be:

- Accurate
- Consistent
- Complete
- Traceable
- Searchable
- Versioned
- Secure
- Reusable

---

# 6. Information Relationships

Information connects:

- Foundation
- Enterprise Architecture
- Capabilities
- Constitution
- Modules
- AI Workforce
- Platforms
- Products
- Source Code

---

# 7. Governance

Information shall have:

- Owner
- Classification
- Access Policy
- Retention Policy
- Version History
- Audit Trail
