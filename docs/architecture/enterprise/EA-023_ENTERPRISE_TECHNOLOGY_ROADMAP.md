# 1. Purpose

Enterprise Technology Roadmap defines the long-term evolution of SBAI technologies, platforms, operating systems, AI workforce, products, hardware, robotics, satellite systems, research, and future innovations.

This roadmap guides technology investments and architectural evolution while maintaining compatibility with the SBAI Constitution and Enterprise Architecture.

---

# 2. Technology Vision

Build an AI-native enterprise capable of designing, engineering, operating, securing, and evolving software and hardware products through an integrated digital workforce under Founder governance.
