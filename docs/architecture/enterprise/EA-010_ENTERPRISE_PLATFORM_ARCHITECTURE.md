# 1. Purpose

Enterprise Platform Architecture defines the common enterprise platforms that provide shared capabilities, services, governance, security, and infrastructure across the SBAI ecosystem.

Platforms are reusable enterprise foundations upon which applications, operating systems, AI agents, and products are built.

---

# 2. Objectives

The platform architecture shall:

- Promote enterprise-wide reuse.
- Eliminate duplicated platform functionality.
- Enable AI-first development.
- Support modular growth.
- Standardize enterprise services.
- Support future technologies.
- Enable global scalability.

---

# 3. Enterprise Platform Portfolio

SBAI consists of the following major enterprise platforms:

- Founder Platform
- Customer Platform
- Enterprise AI Platform
- Knowledge Platform
- Security Platform
- Identity Platform
- Automation Platform
- Workflow Platform
- Integration Platform
- Data Platform
- Infrastructure Platform
- Engineering Platform
- Research Platform
- Analytics Platform
- Operations Platform
- Hardware Platform
- Robotics Platform
- Space Platform

Additional platforms may be introduced without changing the overall enterprise architecture.

---

# 4. Platform Responsibilities

Each platform shall provide:

- Shared enterprise capabilities
- Shared enterprise services
- Standard APIs
- Governance
- Security
- Monitoring
- Documentation
- Versioning

---

# 5. Platform Principles

Every platform shall be:

- Modular
- Independent
- Reusable
- Scalable
- Secure
- Observable
- Extensible
- Constitution compliant

# 7. Security Considerations

Every platform shall implement:

- Identity-aware access control
- Role-based authorization
- Encryption at rest
- Encryption in transit
- Secret management
- Platform monitoring
- Immutable audit logging
- Threat detection
- Secure configuration management
- Business continuity planning

---

# 8. AI Integration

Platforms shall support:

- AI Departments
- Specialist AI Agents
- Enterprise Memory
- Knowledge Graph
- Workflow Engine
- Automation Engine
- Multi-agent orchestration
- AI governance

All AI execution shall comply with constitutional policies.

---

# 9. Governance

Each platform shall define:

- Platform ID
- Owner
- Purpose
- Scope
- Consumers
- Dependencies
- Services
- Security Classification
- Lifecycle
- Version
- Documentation

---

# 10. Future Expansion

The Platform Architecture shall support:

- Thousands of enterprise services
- Thousands of AI agents
- Hundreds of applications
- Multiple operating systems
- Multiple hardware products
- Robotics ecosystems
- Space technologies
- Future AI technologies

without architectural redesign.
