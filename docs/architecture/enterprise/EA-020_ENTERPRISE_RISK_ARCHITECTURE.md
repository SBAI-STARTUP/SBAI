# 1. Purpose

Enterprise Risk Architecture defines the framework for identifying, evaluating, mitigating, monitoring, and governing risks across the SBAI ecosystem.

Risk management applies to business, engineering, AI, infrastructure, security, hardware, robotics, space systems, legal, and future technologies.

---

# 2. Objectives

The enterprise shall:

- Identify risks early
- Reduce business impact
- Protect critical assets
- Improve resilience
- Enable informed decision making
- Support continuous monitoring
- Maintain business continuity

---

# 3. Risk Domains

Enterprise risks include:

- Strategic Risk
- Business Risk
- Financial Risk
- Operational Risk
- Technical Risk
- Software Risk
- Infrastructure Risk
- Cloud Risk
- AI Risk
- Data Risk
- Privacy Risk
- Security Risk
- Supply Chain Risk
- Legal Risk
- Regulatory Risk
- Hardware Risk
- Robotics Risk
- Satellite Risk
- Third-Party Risk
- Reputational Risk

---

# 4. Risk Lifecycle

Every risk follows:

1. Identify
2. Classify
3. Assess
4. Prioritize
5. Mitigate
6. Monitor
7. Review
8. Close

---

# 5. Risk Classification

Risk levels:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Informational

Each risk shall include:

- Probability
- Impact
- Risk Score
- Business Impact
- Technical Impact

---

# 6. Risk Assessment

Every assessment considers:

- Likelihood
- Severity
- Detectability
- Exposure Duration
- Recovery Difficulty
- Cost Impact

---

# 7. Risk Register

Each risk record includes:

- Risk ID
- Title
- Description
- Domain
- Owner
- Severity
- Status
- Mitigation Plan
- Review Date
- Closure Criteria

---

# 8. Risk Treatment

Treatment options:

- Avoid
- Reduce
- Transfer
- Accept

Each decision shall be documented.
