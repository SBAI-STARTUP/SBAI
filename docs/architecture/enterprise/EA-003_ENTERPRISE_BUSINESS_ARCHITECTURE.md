# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Business Architecture of SBAI.

It describes how the enterprise is organized from a business perspective, independent of implementation technologies.

The Business Architecture serves as the blueprint for enterprise capabilities, AI departments, operating systems, platforms, products, and future expansion.

---

# 2. Business Objectives

The business architecture shall:

- Align enterprise strategy with execution.
- Organize enterprise responsibilities.
- Define enterprise domains.
- Support AI-first operations.
- Enable modular business growth.
- Preserve enterprise knowledge.
- Scale without structural redesign.

---

- Products
- Services
- Applications
- Research
- Innovation

- Robotics
- Space Systems

---

# 4. Business Operating Model

The enterprise operates as:

- One Founder
- AI Digital Workforce
- Constitution-driven governance
- Shared enterprise knowledge
- Shared enterprise capabilities
- Shared enterprise platforms

Human employees may be supported in the future without changing the architecture.

---

# 5. Business Capabilities

Every business domain shall expose reusable enterprise capabilities.

Examples include:

- Strategic Planning
- Product Management
- AI Engineering
- Cybersecurity
- Knowledge Management
- Research
- Software Engineering
- Infrastructure Management
- Customer Support
- Business Analytics

Capabilities are formally defined in the Enterprise Capability Framework.

---

# 6. AI Workforce Alignment

Each business domain shall be supported by expert-level AI agents organized into:

- Departments
- Specialist Agents
- Domain Experts
- Coordinators
- Orchestrators

These agents execute business functions under constitutional governance.
