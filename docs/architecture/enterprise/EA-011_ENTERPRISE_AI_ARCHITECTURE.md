# 1. Purpose

Enterprise AI Architecture defines how Artificial Intelligence is organized, governed, secured, orchestrated, and operated throughout SBAI.

AI is considered an enterprise capability rather than a standalone application.

---

# 2. Vision

SBAI shall operate as an AI-native enterprise where expert AI systems collaborate across all enterprise domains while remaining under constitutional governance and Founder oversight.

---

# 3. Objectives

The AI Architecture shall:

- Build an enterprise AI workforce.
- Support expert-level AI departments.
- Enable specialist AI agents.
- Support secure AI execution.
- Enable enterprise reasoning.
- Preserve enterprise memory.
- Support future AGI evolution.
- Maintain constitutional governance.

# 10. AI Security

Every AI component shall implement:

- Identity verification
- Authorization
- Prompt governance
- Tool permission controls

- Secure execution

---

# 11. Constitutional Governance

Every AI system shall obey:

- SBAI Constitution
- Enterprise Policies
- Security Policies
- Engineering Standards
- Founder Authority
- Enterprise Governance

No AI system may bypass constitutional controls.

---

# 12. Founder Oversight

The Founder remains the highest authority.

AI systems may:

- Recommend
- Analyze
- Execute approved actions

AI systems shall not independently modify constitutional documents, enterprise governance, or security policies without Founder approval.
