# Purpose

This document defines the architectural principles that govern all engineering work within SBAI.

Every system, module, service, AI agent, repository, and future product shall follow these principles.

---

# Principles

## AP-001 Documentation First

Every significant engineering artifact shall be documented before implementation.

---

## AP-002 Architecture Before Implementation

Architecture defines implementation—not the reverse.

---

## AP-003 Modular Design

Systems shall be divided into cohesive, loosely coupled modules with clear ownership.

---

## AP-004 Security by Design

Security shall be integrated into architecture from the beginning rather than added later.

---

## AP-005 AI-Native Engineering

AI capabilities are treated as core architectural components where they provide clear value, while preserving human governance for major decisions.

---

## AP-006 Founder Governance

The Founder approves architectural direction and strategic changes.

---

## AP-007 Open Standards

Prefer open, well-documented standards and interoperable technologies when practical.

---

## AP-008 Scalability

Architectures shall support growth in users, products, services, and engineering complexity.

---

## AP-009 Observability

Systems should be designed to support monitoring, logging, auditing, and diagnostics.

---

## AP-010 Evolution

The architecture is expected to evolve through controlled change using Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).
