# Purpose

Founder OS is the central operating platform of SBAI.

It provides the Founder with a single unified workspace to control, monitor, design, build, operate, and evolve the entire SBAI ecosystem.

Founder OS is not a computer operating system like Windows, Linux, or macOS.

It is the operating system for the Founder and the digital enterprise.

Everything inside SBAI ultimately connects to Founder OS.

---

# Mission

To provide one unified platform through which the Founder can control every aspect of SBAI while maintaining complete visibility, governance, security, and decision authority.

Founder OS enables the Founder to operate as:

- Architect
- Engineer
- Researcher
- Product Builder
- Security Leader
- Decision Maker
- Company Owner

using one integrated system.

---

# Vision

Founder OS serves as the digital command center for SBAI.

During Phase 1, it supports a single Founder operating with an unlimited AI workforce.

In future phases, it evolves into the control center for the entire company while preserving Founder authority.

# Constitutional Rules

1. Founder is the sole human operator during Phase 1.

2. AI Workers assist but never replace Founder authority.

3. Every strategic decision remains Founder-controlled.

4. Founder OS is the primary interface for SBAI.

5. No module bypasses Founder governance.

6. Foundation architecture cannot be modified without Founder approval.
