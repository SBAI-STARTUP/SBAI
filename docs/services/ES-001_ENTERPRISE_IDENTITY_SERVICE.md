# ES-001 Enterprise Identity Service

**Service ID:** ES-001

**Service Name:** Enterprise Identity Service

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Approved

**Classification:** Core Enterprise Service

---

# 1. Purpose

Provide secure identity management for every entity inside SBAI.

The Identity Service is the single source of truth for authentication, authorization, and identity lifecycle management.

---

# 2. Mission

Ensure that every interaction inside SBAI is performed by a verified and authorized identity.
