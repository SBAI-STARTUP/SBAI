# ES-008 Enterprise Policy Service

**Service ID:** ES-008

**Service Name:** Enterprise Policy Service

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Approved

**Classification:** Core Enterprise Governance Service

---

# 1. Purpose

Provide centralized policy evaluation and enforcement across the SBAI enterprise.

Every AI, workflow, platform, service, API, infrastructure component, and product must comply with enterprise policies before execution.

---

# 2. Mission

Guarantee that all enterprise actions follow the Constitution, security rules, governance policies, and Founder-defined constraints.
